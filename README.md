# Hi, I'm Otávio Augusto

Software Development Engineer at Amazon, algorithms and data structures
instructor at Ada Tech, and PhD student in Computer Science at the
Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG).

My background combines backend engineering, distributed systems research,
and technical education. I care about turning sound technical designs
into reliable, maintainable software.

### AI-assisted software engineering

I have extensive hands-on experience using AI tools to translate
requirements and technical designs into working systems.

My approach includes iterative feedback loops, and graph-based 
workflows. I structure problems, make constraints explicit, 
and evaluate generated solutions through testing and code review.

I advocate for engineering excellence: clear interfaces, meaningful
automated tests, observability, and careful reasoning about correctness
and failure modes.

### Teaching and research

At Ada Tech, I teach algorithms and data structures, helping students
develop problem-solving skills, analyze complexity, and reason about
implementation trade-offs.

At UFMG, my research explores distributed algorithms, self-adjusting
networks, and reconfigurable data structures. I coauthored research
recognized with the SBRC 2022 Best Paper Award.
